Earth, given wings.

Teluala combines Tellus, the Earth, and ala, a wing. It is a modern name inspired by Latin, not a classical Latin word.

The symbol brings a globe and three wings together. The name, the form, and the engine share one idea: lightness, speed, and focus.

A lighter, faster globe engine for the web.

Choose your background.

Use the black logo on a light surface and the white logo on a dark surface. The SVG artwork is vector-based, with no external fonts or embedded images.

Give it space.

Keep at least 5% of the symbol width as clear space around the logo.

Keep it readable.

Use the horizontal logo at 160px or wider. Below that, use the symbol.

Keep the form.

Do not alter the proportions, the globe and wings, or their relation to the wordmark.
